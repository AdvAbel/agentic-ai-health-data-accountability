# Agentic AI Accountability & Health Data Checklist

A research project examining legal accountability gaps that arise when autonomous ("agentic") AI systems process sensitive health data — with a practical self-assessment checklist for spotting attribution and oversight gaps in a deployed system.

This project is part of an LL.M. thesis in data privacy and information technology law. It is a work in progress and will grow as the research does.

## Why this exists

Agentic AI systems don't just respond to instructions — they plan, retrieve data, choose actions, and act with limited real-time human input. Existing data protection law (in India and elsewhere) was written assuming a human or organisation fixes the purpose and means of processing in advance. This project documents where that assumption breaks down for health data specifically, and offers a practical checklist for spotting the gap in a real deployment.

## What's in this repository

- **`/docs`** — the legal analysis: the accountability chain, competing liability models, a clause-by-clause stress test against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025, and comparative notes against the GDPR and EU AI Act.
- **`/checklist`** — a simple, self-contained webpage for assessing an agentic AI system's accountability and privacy risk when it handles health data.

## What this project is NOT

- Not legal advice.
- Not a certified compliance, audit, or regulatory tool.
- Not backed by any user statistics, adoption figures, or case studies — none are claimed, and none should be assumed.

This is an evolving academic research resource. Feedback and corrections are welcome via GitHub Issues.
